# D74 — Quyền sử dụng của luồng voice hiện tại

**Purpose:** Review the exact existing voice lane and its deployment obligations, separating personal local integration from redistribution. This is a source-backed engineering review, not a new model selection or an unconditional legal clearance.

**Date:** 2026-11-05. **Disposition:** prepare an opt-in, source-only, personal-local integration with the current artifacts. Keep binary/model redistribution and final product activation separately gated. No identified publisher statement requires replacing the current preset for this proposed scope.

For MIT-covered copies: preserve copyright/permission text. Packaging still needs the actual versions' notices and any GPL/LGPL/vendor conditions. ## Scope disposition

- **A — recommended:** personal-local, opt-in source integration using these already present artifacts. Keep published notices/provenance; no bundled wheels/binaries/weights, external hosting, new cloning or added voices. Published rights support preparing this path; activation still needs the owner's explicit product decision and technical gates.
- **B — separate future distribution:** complete an actual shipping SBOM/notice pack, denoiser provenance-to-notice mapping, native-library/vendor redistribution review, and a project license decision. No repository-level LICENSE/NOTICE was found in the checked root or `ai/`. Do not silently choose one for the owner.
- Piper/VAIS/Lessac issues remain in their historical candidate records. They are not evidence that this distinct Turbo/preset path inherits the same restriction; no Piper deletion, replacement or new evaluation follows.
- Custom voice enrollment, additional languages and commercial/public service delivery are outside this proposal. Keep the stopped pronunciation cycles and the existing model-registry strategy.
