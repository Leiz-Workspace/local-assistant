[Transition analysis](../progress/QWEN_E5_COMPARISON_AND_TRANSITION.md) and the [complete proposed configuration with exact E5 rollback](../evidence/model-eval-001-transition-proposal.json) define a separate1024-dimensional generation and full reindex, source/ACL/count/hash fencing, cutover abort conditions and restoration of the original E5 configuration/routing. These are proposals, not applied changes. Current E5 selection, original D24 evidence and commit a88584b remain unchanged. D25 remains NEXT.
