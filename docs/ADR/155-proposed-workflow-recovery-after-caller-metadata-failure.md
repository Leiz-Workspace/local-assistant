Keep the six invalid first slots as history; never merge them into the replacement quality/performance denominator.

A hallucinated negative remains a failure. Measure submission→complete PCM as a software proxy; it is not physical first-audible timing or a four-second product PASS. No new voice-listening labels are generated by this campaign.
